## Contribution Guidelines

- Each member works on their assigned feature using an individual feature branch.
- All contributions must be submitted through pull requests.
- Pull requests must be reviewed and approved by at least one teammate before merging.
- Members should provide meaningful commit messages.
- Each member is responsible for documenting their contributions.
